## What is Klinko Content Strategy Builder?

Klinko Content Strategy Builder is an AI market research skill for turning audience needs, questions, motivations, and decision stages into a focused content plan. It helps teams select themes, angles, formats, and priorities that serve a defined audience and business goal instead of producing disconnected topics or generic publishing calendars.

## What this skill helps you do

- Connect content themes to a specific audience and goal
- Prioritize questions and topics by strategic relevance
- Choose useful angles, formats, and funnel stages
- Create a coherent roadmap rather than a list of random ideas

## Questions you can ask

- “What content should we create for this audience?”
- “Build a content strategy for our product launch.”
- “Which topics match customer demand and buying questions?”
- “Create a three-month content roadmap for this market.”

## What you receive

| Deliverable | Decision it supports |
|---|---|
| Content pillars | Create a coherent editorial territory |
| Topic and question map | Address what the audience wants to understand |
| Format recommendations | Match content form to intent |
| Priority roadmap | Decide what to publish first and why |

## Example research process

1. Describe the product, idea, audience, or market you want to understand.
2. Add relevant public context such as geography, category, goals, or constraints.
3. Ask a clear decision question rather than requesting generic research.
4. Review the structured answer and use follow-up questions to refine the decision.

### Example prompt

> Build a content strategy for a cybersecurity consultancy targeting operations leaders at growing healthcare companies. The goal is qualified inbound demand.

### Example result structure

- Audience-specific content pillars
- Questions and themes mapped to decision stages
- Recommended formats and distribution angles
- A prioritized roadmap tied to the business goal
